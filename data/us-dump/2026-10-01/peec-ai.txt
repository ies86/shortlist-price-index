FAQs

Get answers to the most common questions about AI search and Peec AI.

What are AI answers?
AI answers are the individual AI search results we track across platforms. It represents one chat result per model. Example: If you are running 25 prompts across 3 models for 30 days, we would analyze 25x3x30 = 2250 AI answers.
Can i track multiple sub-brands?
Yes. You can set up and monitor multiple sub-brands within your account, ensuring each gets its own visibility tracking. Agency accounts have this per default. Schedule a call with us to set it up for your brand as well.
Can I split the amount of prompts across multiple projects or brands?
Prompts can be shared across your account, allowing you to allocate it to different projects or brands as needed.
Can the prompt amount be adjusted?
Yes. You can upgrade or adjust your prompt volume at any time to match your usage and growth.
What determines the pricing?
Pricing is based on the number of tracked prompts and models analyzed. Higher tiers get discounts on price per prompt and a dedicated support function.
How does pricing work for multiple countries/languages?
You can track queries across any supported country or language with no additional cost. Pricing remains tied to usage of prompts, not the number of regions or languages.
Is there a yearly discount?
We offer a 15% discount for customers who choose annual billing.
How do agency bundles work if we mange several clients?
Agencies can manage multiple client projects under one bundle, with centralized billing and flexible prompt allocation.
