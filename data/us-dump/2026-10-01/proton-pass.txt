What makes Proton Pass different?
Protected by end-to-end encryption

Your data is secured with the same battle-tested, zero-knowledge, end-to-end encryption as other Proton services. Your data is further protected by some of the world's strongest data privacy laws, thanks to our Swiss jurisdiction.

Available anywhere you need it

Proton Pass has apps for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux. It is also available through web app and browser extensions, including Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, Brave and more.

Sign in faster with Proton Pass

Proton Pass recognizes the websites and apps you use and autofills forms with your credentials on any browser or device. No need to copy and paste.

Hide-my-email aliases

Whenever you sign up for a new online account, Proton Pass can automatically create an alias to keep your actual email address protected. 

Paid feature
Integrated 2FA authenticator

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is one of the best ways to protect your accounts online.

Proton Pass makes 2FA easier with an integrated authenticator that stores your 2FA codes and automatically displays and autofills them.

Paid feature
Secure sharing with anyone

Easily share sensitive information securely with anyone, even if they don't use Proton Pass. You can share any single item, or the whole vault.

Paid feature
Dark Web Monitoring

Receive immediate alerts if your personal information is compromised by a data leak. You can also add custom domains and external email addresses to be monitored.

Advanced account protection

Our high-security program, Proton Sentinel, combines AI with human expertise to prevent hackers from gaining access to your accounts, even if they have your passwords.
