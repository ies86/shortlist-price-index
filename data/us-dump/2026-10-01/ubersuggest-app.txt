Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my account at any time?

Yes, you can cancel your account or downgrade to a free account at any time.

What type of payments do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards as well as debit cards. Including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, JCB and UnionPay. We also accept PayPal.

Why do I have to sign in with Google?

This allows us access to your Google Search Console account which allows us to find more errors and issues with your site that need to be fixed.

Are there any contracts, setup fees, or hidden fees?

There are no setup fees or hidden fees. If you choose the monthly option, there are no contracts. If you pick a lifetime plan, it is a one-time payment for lifetime access to Ubersuggest.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a one-time 7-day free trial for monthly subscriptions. Lifetime plan purchases are final and non-refundable. We recommend using the free trial to explore Ubersuggest before opting for a lifetime plan.

Can I get an invoice for my subscription under my company name?

Yes, you can. Just send us an email at support@neilpatel.com and we will take care of this.

Can I get API access?

We don’t have any API access plans available at the moment.

How accurate is Ubersuggest data?

We use multiple data providers to come up with estimations. Our data providers are similar to the same ones other SEO tools use.
