What all plans include
No matter which plan you choose - Tresorit offers you an unbeatable combination of protection and usability. We ensure that your data remains secure and under your control at all times.
End-to-end encrypted file storage to protect your data from unauthorized access, even when it is stored in the cloud.
Zero-Knowledge system for maximum privacy and trust ensuring that even Tresorit cannot see your data.
Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) to provide an extra layer of security against unauthorized access, especially if passwords are compromised.
Granular Permissions to allow precise control over access rights to files and folders.
Multi-Platform Support to ensure access to Tresorit offline and online on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android and via the web.

FAQ Tresorit SecureCloud for Private Use
Can I try Tresorit for free?
Yes, of course! You can try any of our plans for free, for 14 days without any limitations.
What payment methods can I use?
We accept payments via credit card and PayPal. You can choose your preferred method during the checkout process.
Why do I need to provide my payment information for the trial period?
For security reasons, payment information is required even during the trial phase. It also facilitates the transition after the trial period, as the payment details do not need to be entered again.
How can I decide which plan is right for me?
For a proper decision, you can compare all features. Please use our feature list, which you can download here.
Can I change plans?
Subscription owners on paid plans can switch between plans easily.
Navigate to Billing.
Under Plan details, click Modify plan.
Head to Plan type, then click Apply changes.
Click Compare features to read more about the packages.
After choosing your new plan, click Upgrade.
What assistance do I get if something doesn't work?
If something doesn't work, you can contact our free support via email or chat.
How can I cancel my subscription and what happens after?
You can cancel your subscription and downgrade to Basic any time.
Head to your Admin Center/Account Portal.
Navigate to Billing.
Select Modify plan.
Click Cancel subscription.
Before you finish, let us know your reasons for cancelling your plan.
